Upload the e-Aadhaar PDF and get one A4 page with the full Aadhaar letter on top and the cut-out card (front and back) at real ID-card size below. The photo is brightened and sharpened so it prints clearly on glossy photo paper. Download a 300 dpi JPEG or PDF, or print directly.

Click to choose the e-Aadhaar PDF, or drop it here The PDF downloaded from myAadhaar / UIDAI works best (a JPG or PNG scan also works, without photo enhancement).

Step 3 · Check the A4 page, then download or print

Printing on glossy photo paper

  • Paper size A4, paper type Glossy Photo Paper, quality Best / High.
  • Scale 100% / Actual size — not "Fit to page" or "Fill", or the card won't come out at the real 85.6 × 54 mm size.
  • Let the ink dry for a few minutes before cutting along the dashed guides.
  • The photo inside an e-Aadhaar is small (about 160 × 200 pixels), so enhancement makes it clearer but it can't become as sharp as a camera photo.
  • A printout of e-Aadhaar is valid as proof of identity, the same as the original letter. Do not alter the printed details.
