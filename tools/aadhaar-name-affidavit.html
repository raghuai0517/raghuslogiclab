Format per UIDAI E&U-I SOP dated 18.09.2026 (Annexures B–F)

Do you need an affidavit at all? (from Annexure A)

  • No affidavit: minor changes such as initials expanded, middle name added or dropped, word order changed, small spelling fixes. Submit your ID proof (PoI / PoR).
  • No document at all: removing dots (R.P. Singh → R P Singh) or fixing random capital letters (RaMesh → Ramesh).
  • Gazette notification only: a complete change of name or identity (Ram Gupta → Shyam Gupta). No affidavit here will work for this.
  • Affidavit needed: data entry error (B / C), surname change after marriage or divorce (D), removing “urf” or “alias” (E / F). Always attach your PoI / PoR as well.
