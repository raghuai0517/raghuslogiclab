Independent tool — not from the Census organisation. RaghusLogicLab is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India, or any government office. It only prints a paper working sheet from your own Abridged Houselist; the official record is what you enter in the PE App. Your files are read only in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere. Official site: censusindia.gov.in.

Upload the Abridged Houselist (AHL) PDF of your Houselisting Block and get a compact Household Schedule for every household: A4 landscape, 4 pages per household (Q1–10 on page 1, Q11–40 on pages 2–4, one column per member). Add the CMMS Excel too and each household gets exactly as many member slots as it has persons (maximum 6).

1. Choose your files

Choose the AHL PDF to begin.

Filled in for you (in blue)

  • State, District, Sub-district, Village/Town, Ward, EB and Sub-EB codes; Hamlet / Locality
  • Building No., Census House No., Household No., Type of Household
  • Head of household's name as member 1; with the Excel also sex, SC / ST (10a / 10b) and mobile number (Q36)

Temples, vacant and non-residential census houses are left out. Everything else stays blank for you to fill in the field.

  1. Open print view opens all schedules in a new tab. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (on a phone: Chrome).
  2. Click Print / Save as PDF at the top of that tab (or press Ctrl+P).
  3. Keep Margins: Default and Scale: 100, and tick Background graphics. Print, or choose Save as PDF.
  4. To print later or on another computer, use Download print file and open that file in Chrome or Edge.
