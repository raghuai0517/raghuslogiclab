Print on A4, portrait, scale 100%, with Background graphics on. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer. Each notice has a tear-off acknowledgement for the pattadar's signature and the mode of service.

How to use and what each check means ఎలా వాడాలి
  • Download the correlation statement of the village as Excel and upload it. Titles like "Old Sy.No", "RSR Extent", "LP No", "LP Extent", "Correlated Extent" and the pattadar columns are found by themselves; check them in step 2.
  • Clear the errors in step 3 before the statement goes to the Tahsildar. Warnings need a look at the records (sale, succession, mutation papers) but may be correct.
  • Notices are made from the same data. By default an LP with an open error gets no notice, so a wrong extent is never served.
  • The appeal period and appellate authority print as you enter them. Fill them as per the current orders for Resurvey 2.0; leave the days blank to write them by hand.
  • Keep the notice register (Excel) to record the date and mode of service and the acknowledgement for each notice.
Missing value
No survey number, LP number, old extent or LP extent. A survey number with no LP has not been correlated.
Extent not a number
The cell can't be read as an area, or is zero or negative. Acre–cent entries such as 1-25 are read as 1.25 Ac.
Old extent differs between rows
The same survey number (with sub-division) has two different old extents.
LP extent differs between rows
The same LP has two different LP extents.
Survey no. extent does not match
Old extent differs from the new extent correlated to it by more than the allowed difference.
LP parts do not add up
An LP made from several survey numbers has parts whose total differs from the LP extent.
Part extent needed
An LP covers more than one survey number but no correlated extent is given.
Duplicate row
The same survey number, LP, pattadar and extent appear more than once.
Pattadar name differs
The pattadar after resurvey doesn't resemble any old pattadar of that survey number. Initials and word order are allowed for; Government land is skipped.
Classification differs
One LP is marked with more than one land classification.
Several pattadars on one LP
For information: joint holdings to confirm against the 1B.
LP numbers not in statement
For information: gaps in the LP number sequence.
