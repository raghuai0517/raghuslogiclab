Aadhaar Services Report for AP Seva

For Aadhaar operators at village and ward secretariats: upload the CSV exported from the Aadhaar UC Client and get a day-wise list of services done, using the same "Type of Service" names as the AP Seva portal's Digital Services › Challan Payment form. Print it on A4 and tick off each day as you enter it.

Free · no login · nothing is uploaded

Step 1 · Upload the UC Client CSV

Click to choose CSV files, or drop them here You can select several exports at once. Entries repeated across files are counted once, keeping the final status (Completed or Rejected).

🔒 The files are read only inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved on any server.

How services are matched to the portal

  • New enrolment (type N) → Aadhaar enrolment
  • Mandatory biometric update, ₹0 → Mandatory Biometric update along with Demographic updates (Age 5-7 && 15-17), or Ages 7-15 if you choose that in Step 2
  • Mandatory biometric update, ₹125 → Mandatory Biometric Update For Greater Than 17 Years
  • Update, ₹125 → Biometric Update with or without Demographic update · Update, ₹75 → Demographic Update
  • The UC Client export doesn't record the resident's age, so change any entry on the Enrolment Details tab if it belongs elsewhere. The date and time come from the enrolment number.
