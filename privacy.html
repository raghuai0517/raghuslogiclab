Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

RaghusLogicLab (operated by P. Raghu Sreenivas) respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your information on raghuslogiclab.com.

Details you type into the tools

Most tools run entirely in your browser (the AP DSC 2026 Exam Hall account is described below). Names, addresses, Aadhaar numbers and other details you type are not sent to or stored on our servers; closing the page clears them.

Payments

Payments (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets) are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment status, amount and payment ID; we never see your card details or UPI PIN. The payment ID is added to the PDF's file properties so a payment can be matched if you contact us.

When you pay through Razorpay's checkout on our tool pages, only the tool name and the document type are sent with the order. The details you typed in the form are not sent to us or to Razorpay. So that your download can finish if your phone reloads the page after the UPI app, those details are kept in that browser tab only, and are cleared once the PDF is saved or the tab is closed.

AP DSC 2026 Exam Hall (website and Android app)

The AP DSC 2026 Exam Hall has a free trial (on the website and in the Android app “AP DSC Exam Hall”) and a paid full version on the website. Your test answers, scores and settings are saved only on your own device.

Account (full version). When you sign up or sign in with Google, we receive your name, Gmail address and profile picture from Google. You also give us your mobile number, district and the post you are preparing for. We use these only to run your account, show your access period and contact you about it.

Payments. Payments for full access are handled by Razorpay. We keep the payment ID, amount, date and the number of access days.

Sign-in log. For security, each sign-in, new account, payment and admin sign-in is logged with the time, the approximate city and region worked out from the internet address, and the device type (for example “Android · Chrome”).

Free trial and Android app usage. The free trial does not ask who you are. It creates a random visitor number on your device and sends us, at most a few times a day: that number, the post you chose, how many tests you started and how often you tapped a locked item, plus the approximate city and region (from the internet address) and the device type. We use this only to see how the free trial is used. If you later create an account on the same device, the visitor number is linked to your account.

Where it is kept. This information is stored on Cloudflare (our server provider) and is sent over encrypted connections. Logs and visitor records are deleted automatically after 180 days. Account records are kept while your account is in use.

Your choices. To see or delete your account data, email us from your Gmail address. To stop the trial usage reports, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. The app is not intended for children under 13.

This is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Government of Andhra Pradesh or its School Education Department. Official information: cse.ap.gov.in and tet2dsc.apcfss.in.

Contact messages

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The Project Studio page may store drafts in your own browser's local storage.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only where required by law or to complete a payment you make.
