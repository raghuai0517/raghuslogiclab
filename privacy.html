Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

RaghusLogicLab (operated by P. Raghu Sreenivas) respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your information on raghuslogiclab.com.

Details you type into the tools

The tools run entirely in your browser. Names, addresses, Aadhaar numbers and other details you type are not sent to or stored on our servers; closing the page clears them.

Payments

Payments (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets) are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment status, amount and payment ID; we never see your card details or UPI PIN. The payment ID is added to the PDF's file properties so a payment can be matched if you contact us.

When you pay through Razorpay's checkout on our tool pages, only the tool name and the document type are sent with the order. The details you typed in the form are not sent to us or to Razorpay. So that your download can finish if your phone reloads the page after the UPI app, those details are kept in that browser tab only, and are cleared once the PDF is saved or the tab is closed.

Contact messages

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The Project Studio page may store drafts in your own browser's local storage.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only where required by law or to complete a payment you make.
