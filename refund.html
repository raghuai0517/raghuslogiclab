Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers everything sold on raghuslogiclab.com: paid PDF downloads (₹10 per PDF), Pahani village reports, and AP DSC 2026 Exam Hall full access.

Before you pay

You can fill in the form and check the full A4 preview free, so please make sure the details are correct before paying.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time before paying by closing the payment box. Once the PDF has been downloaded, the order is complete and cannot be cancelled.

Refunds

  • Paid but no PDF: if you paid and the PDF did not download, first reopen the tool page in the same browser (it finishes the download within 2 hours); otherwise email us with your Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). We will send the PDF or refund ₹10.
  • Charged twice: the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Because the product is a digital file delivered instantly, we do not refund PDFs that downloaded correctly, including for typing mistakes made by the customer.

Pahani village reports

  • Paid but no report: reopen the tool in the same browser — the report can be downloaded again free of charge for 24 hours after payment. If it still fails, email us with your payment ID and we will send the report or refund the payment.
  • Reports that downloaded correctly are not refunded, because the file is delivered instantly.

AP DSC 2026 Exam Hall full access

  • Paid but access not active: sign in again with the same Google account; if access is still locked, email us with your payment ID and we will activate it or refund the payment in full.
  • Once full access is active, the payment is not refundable, because all content becomes available immediately. The free trial lets you check the Exam Hall before paying.
  • Access suspended for sharing or resale is not refunded.

Timeline

Please contact us within 7 days of payment. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

How to ask

Email raghuslogiclab@gmail.com with the date, amount and payment reference.
